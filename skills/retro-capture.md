# name
retro-capture

# intent
Capture execution outcomes, incidents, and process improvements to accelerate future delivery quality and predictability.

# trigger_conditions
- Task, sprint, or release completion.
- Incident, rollback, or unexpected blocker encountered.
- Repeated failure pattern appears across cycles.

# required_inputs
- Delivered scope and acceptance results.
- Quality and release-readiness reports.
- Timeline of issues, decisions, and mitigations.

# procedure
1. Summarize what was planned vs what was delivered.
2. Record what worked well and why.
3. Record what failed or slowed progress and root causes.
4. Define concrete improvements with owners and target cycle.
5. Capture reusable playbook updates for skills and rules.

# validation
- Retro outcomes are actionable and specific.
- Improvement actions are measurable and assigned.
- Lessons learned are linked to actual events, not assumptions.

# expected_outputs
- Structured retrospective summary.
- Prioritized improvement actions.
- Updates proposed for skills/rules/process docs.

# failure_modes
- Generic retrospective notes with no ownership.
- Root causes confused with symptoms.
- Lessons not propagated into reusable skills.

# handoff_notes
- Feed improvement actions into backlog and next sprint planning.
- Update `skills/` and relevant `WARP.md` files when a new stable practice emerges.
