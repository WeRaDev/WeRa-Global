## Customer Segments

### Segment 1: Ideological Prosumers
- **Profile**: Eco-conscious individuals, values-driven, 30–50 age range
- **Motivation**: Energy sovereignty, data privacy, environmental impact
- **Channel**: Social media, sustainability communities, direct web (wera.global)
- **Messaging**: Fear-based (energy dependence, Big Tech surveillance) + aspiration (ownership, community power)
- **Products**: Full SolarSeed + WERA Cloud + token participation

### Segment 2: Cost-Driven Local Businesses / SMEs
- **Profile**: Small-to-medium enterprises seeking cost reduction
- **Motivation**: Lower energy bills, reduce operational costs, business continuity
- **Channel**: Ambassador referrals, B2B direct sales, installation partner network
- **Messaging**: ROI-focused ("pay less than your current bill"), resilience ("never lose power")
- **Products**: SolarSeed leasing primarily; WERA Cloud as add-on

### Segment 3: General Public (Future)
- **Profile**: Mainstream consumers, not yet sustainability-focused
- **Motivation**: Cost savings, convenience, community belonging
- **Channel**: Mass market (TBD)
- **Messaging**: Strategy pending
- **Status**: Not yet activated; requires validated funnel from Segments 1+2

## Sales Pipeline

### 11-Step Online Sales Journey

1. Visitor arrives at wera.global (map or calculator entry point)
2. Explores interactive solar map (see Map Station Types below)
3. Uses solar calculator / proposal calculator (enters electricity bill data)
4. Receives personalized energy proposal (minimum 20% savings guaranteed)
5. **Signs Letter of Intent (LOI) via email** — confirms data accuracy, property authority, and intent to proceed (template: `templates/LOI-SolarSeed-Template.md`)
6. Creates account / registers interest (€1 symbolic pre-order)
7. Sales team follow-up (dual-proposal strategy: energy-only OR full ecosystem)
8. Site assessment by installation partner (Iberia Renew Engineering)
9. Technical proposal delivered
10. Contract signed (leasing agreement)
11. Installation and onboarding to WERA Cloud

## Customer Acquisition Channels

### Channel 1: Organic / Digital
- wera.global website with calculator and map
- Social media presence (LinkedIn, X/Twitter — small following currently)
- Content marketing around energy sovereignty narrative
- Emergency positioning: SolarSeed launched as response to April 2025 blackout

### Channel 2: Partner-Embedded (B2B2C)
- WERA Cloud embedded in partner platforms ("Embedded Sovereign Cloud" strategy)
- Partners (Soula, Ubbu, WiFi Map) bring users
- Users discover WeRa through partner services
- CAC ≈ €0 from this channel

### Channel 3: Batch Acquisition via Associations
- Work with **farmer associations** and **landlord associations** (membership-based organizations)
- Arrange batch presentations: "working with several customers at the same time"
- Target: associations with 20+ local members for batch site assessments
- Municipality connections for grant/subsidy access
- Includes regeneration villages / eco-villages
- Pipeline includes "several municipalities and associations and regeneration villages"

### Channel 4: Secondary Lead Market
- Purchase pre-qualified solar leads from aggregators at €100–200 per lead
- These are people who contacted other solar companies, received a proposal, found it too expensive
- "Secondary market" leads — already aggregated, already paid for by the original company
- Cheaper than fresh leads (€100–200 vs €250–400 for exclusive fresh leads in EU)
- Also building an **AI-based lead generation engine** ("lead generation robot") — budgeted in CapEx as AI-based SMM and Sales engine

#### EU Solar Lead Market Context
- Shared solar leads (EU): €20–50 per lead, sold to 3–5 installers ([Phantom Digital](https://phantomdigital.co.uk/how-much-do-solar-leads-cost/))
- Exclusive solar leads (EU): €50–150 residential, €200–300 commercial
- US benchmark: $50–$400 per lead depending on state and exclusivity ([LinkedIn Solar Lead Guide 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/solar-lead-cost-guide-2026-what-installation-pay-leads-mihajlovic-cum2e))
- Customer Acquisition Cost (full funnel): $3,000–$7,000 residential in US; WeRa targets €283.25 blended (20× lower than industry)
- Aged/secondary leads: as low as €5–20 (30+ days old), conversion rate lower but cost dramatically reduced
- WeRa's €100–200 secondary lead range is within normal EU market pricing

### Channel Readiness Constraints (Execution Sequencing)

| Channel | Readiness | Dependency | Immediate Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Installer backlogs / declined clients | **Ready now** | None beyond outreach scripts and installer contacts | Call installers and request declined upfront-cost cases |
| Associations / municipalities / SME batch entry | **Partially blocked** | Legal entity credibility and trust signal | Prioritize legal registration and institution-facing materials |
| Partner-embedded B2B2C cloud | **Strategic, not immediate** | Formal partner activation and technical integration | Keep as medium-term scale layer, not first execution bet |

Sequencing rule: run installer-backlog channel immediately while preparing institutional channel prerequisites in parallel.
