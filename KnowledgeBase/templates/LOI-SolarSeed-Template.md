## PART II — RECITALS

The Parties set out the following as background to this Letter of Intent:

**A.** On [DATE], the Customer visited the Company's platform at **wera.global** and used the FilantropiaSolar Solar Calculator (hereinafter "the Calculator"), a proprietary tool designed to provide preliminary energy assessments for prospective SolarSeed programme participants.

**B.** The Calculator generated a preliminary energy proposal (hereinafter "the Preliminary Proposal") based on electricity consumption data entered by the Customer, including information derived from the Customer's electricity bill(s). The Preliminary Proposal is set out in summary form in Part V of this document.

**C.** Based on the results of the Preliminary Proposal, the Customer has expressed an interest in proceeding to a detailed technical and engineering site assessment of the property identified herein, with a view to entering into a formal SolarSeed leasing agreement.

**D.** The Parties wish to record the Customer's intent and the Company's obligations at this pre-contractual stage by means of this Letter of Intent, which is entered into in good faith and in accordance with the principles of pre-contractual liability under Portuguese law (*culpa in contrahendo*, Article 227 of the Portuguese Civil Code).

## PART VII — DATA PROTECTION

**7.1 GDPR Compliance**
The Company processes the Customer's personal data, including electricity bill data and the information contained in this Letter of Intent, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (the General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR"), as applicable in Portugal through Law No. 58/2019 of 8 August 2019.

**7.2 Encryption and Storage**
All personal data and electricity bill data provided by the Customer through the FilantropiaSolar platform, as well as data contained in this document, will be encrypted in transit and at rest, and stored on secure servers in accordance with the Company's data protection policies and applicable GDPR standards.

**7.3 Purpose Limitation**
The Customer's personal data will be used **solely** for the purpose of:

- Evaluating the suitability of the property for a SolarSeed installation;
- Preparing the technical and financial proposal referenced in Part VI;
- Managing the pre-contractual relationship described in this Letter of Intent.

The data will not be shared with third parties except where strictly necessary for the above purposes (e.g., with the engineering assessment partner), and will not be used for marketing or other purposes without the Customer's separate consent.

**7.4 Customer Rights**
Pursuant to Articles 15–22 of the GDPR, the Customer has the right to:

- Access the personal data held about them;
- Request rectification of inaccurate data;
- Request erasure of their data ("right to be forgotten") at any time;
- Object to processing or request restriction of processing;
- Lodge a complaint with the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), the competent supervisory authority in Portugal.

To exercise any of the above rights, the Customer may contact the Company's Data Protection contact at: **[DPO email / privacy@wera.global]**.
