## Portugal Solar Energy Market

### Market Size and Growth
- **Installed capacity (2026)**: 8.19 GW — on track to reach 20.65 GW by 2031 ([Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/portugal-solar-energy-market))
- **CAGR (2026–2031)**: 20.31% capacity growth
- **Market value (2024)**: USD 3.89 billion → projected USD 9.03 billion by 2032 (11.1% CAGR) ([Verified Market Research](https://www.verifiedmarketresearch.com/product/portugal-solar-energy-market/))
- **Residential segment CAGR**: 24.3% — expanding faster than utility-scale
- **Prosumers**: 157,000 and growing (98% residential installation growth by 2023 base)

### Renewable Energy Performance (2025)
- Renewable generation: record **37 TWh** — met **68% of national electricity demand** ([Strategic Energy Europe](https://strategicenergy.eu/renewables-set-new-record-in-portugal-covering-nearly-70-of-power-demand-in-2025/))
- Solar accounted for **11% of consumption** (up 25% year-on-year)
- Total electricity consumption: 53.1 TWh (3.2% YoY increase)
- EDP (Portugal's largest utility): 44% profit increase in 2025; plans to add 2 GW new capacity

### Key Projects
- **Alqueva Floating Solar Park** (EDP): 5 MW, 12,000 panels on reservoir
- **Beja Agro-Photovoltaic Park**: 123 MW, largest agri-voltaic in Europe
- **Torre Bela**: 272 MWp
- **Morgavel** (RWE): 46 MWac, first RWE asset in Portugal

### Grid Infrastructure
- Portugal committed **€400M** to grid strengthening (31-point package)
- National BESS auction planned by January 2026
- €25M for solar PV + backup batteries at critical infrastructure
- "Green map" for pre-approved renewable energy zones
- Goal: exceed 80% renewables by 2026

## Portugal Energy Regulation

### Decree-Law 15/2022
- Establishes legal framework for Portugal's National Electricity System (SEN)
- Introduces support for decentralized energy production
- Defines **Renewable Energy Communities (RECs)** and **Citizen Energy Communities (CECs)**
- Self-consumption tiers by installed power:
  - ≤350 W: no prior control required
  - 350 W–30 kW: prior communication
  - 30 kW–1 MW: prior registration + operation certificate
  - >1 MW: production + operation license
- Grid operator consultation required above certain thresholds

Sources: [EU Clean Energy Islands](https://clean-energy-islands.ec.europa.eu/countries/portugal/legal/community-energy-framework/energy-communities-energy-cooperatives-energy), [Chambers](https://chambers.com/articles/portugal-new-legal-framework-of-the-sen-decree-law-no-15-2022-of-14-january-self-consumption-2), [MLGTS PDF](https://www.mlgts.pt/xms/files/site_2018/guias/2024/The_new_legal_framework_for_the_electricity_sector_-_Comments_on_Decree-Law_no._15-2022_of_14_January.pdf)

### WeRa's Regulatory Position
- **No energy license required** — WeRa leases equipment for energy self-hosting, which falls under self-consumption rules
- Future plan: when excess energy is available to sell, will obtain energy community status and license under DL 15/2022
- This is a deliberate regulatory strategy to avoid licensing delays during early growth

## Solar Economics (Europe)

### LCOE (Levelized Cost of Electricity)
- Solar PV LCOE (2025): **US$39/MWh** (6% increase from 2024, but still cheapest source) ([PV Tech](https://www.pv-tech.org/solar-lcoe-anomous-6-rise-between-2024-2025/))
- CCGT (gas): US$102/MWh
- Nuclear: US$258/MWh
- Solar LCOE fell **97% between 2010 and 2025** — sharpest decline of any technology
- BESS LCOE fell 93% over same period

### Grid Price Spread (Portugal)
- Retail grid prices: ~€88/MWh wholesale average (2025)
- Solar LCOE: ~€20–30/MWh
- Spread ratio: ~3–4× (favorable for self-consumption economics)
- Homeowners monetize retail tariffs nearly 2× as much as wholesale

### Challenges
- **Curtailment**: Portugal curtailed ~200 GWh in 2024 (enough for 60,000 homes)
- **Negative prices**: Europe facing surge in negative power prices as solar output grows
- **Grid congestion**: new solar coming online faster than grid can expand
- BNEF forecasts 2026 may be first year with fewer new solar projects than previous year
