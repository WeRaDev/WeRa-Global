## Non-negotiable constraints
1. Do not replace current KB operating baseline with full AI-native stack in one step.
2. Do not activate DAO/SBT governance automation before business KPI stabilization.
3. Keep AI runtime to low-risk action scope until stabilization gates are achieved.
4. Keep unresolved-question governance discipline and explicit owner/action traceability.
## KPI stabilization gates (promotion criteria)
Autonomy and advanced governance scopes may be expanded only after all three thresholds are met and verified:
- Customer pipeline: `>= 10` active qualified opportunities
- CapEx funding secured: `>= €500000`
- Income flow stabilized: `>= €500/month`
Verification evidence must be attached to governance review prior to promotion decision.
## AI action boundary (pre-stabilization)
Allowed:
- lead generation support,
- customer outreach assistance,
- draft generation for human review.
Not allowed:
- autonomous commitments, pricing approvals, payment actions, contract execution, or irreversible operations.
## Controlled hybrid services policy
Hybrid services are allowed where ROI is clear and governance approves the trade-off.
Each approval package must include:
1. ROI hypothesis with target metric and decision horizon.
2. Sovereignty impact classification (none/low/medium/high).
3. Data sensitivity classification and handling boundary.
4. Rollback plan and accountable owner.
5. Exit criteria for continuation or decommission.
