# Miguel Counsel Memo — Track A: Torres Vedras Retrospective Qualification

> This memo is about **what already happened** in Torres Vedras. It is not asking counsel to validate the future repeatable WeRa baseline, and it must not be used as proof of grid-connected UPAC / final-consumer Track B.

## 1. Core Ask

Please help us characterise the current legal status of the Torres Vedras pilot and identify the minimum written document(s) that should be signed now to truthfully memorialise the arrangement and reduce legal ambiguity.

We need to understand:

- what the current arrangement legally is;
- what risks arise from the verbal / informal state;
- what can be signed now without misrepresenting the past;
- what facts or evidence you need before giving a definitive answer.

## 2. Why This Is Separate From Track B

Torres Vedras is not a clean pilot of the future selected model.

It is currently classified as:

> `customer installation with incomplete commercial wrapper`

It is a real installed object, but it does not prove a repeatable `fixed service fee + operator` commercial baseline.

It also does not prove the ordinary future legal object:

- grid-connected UPAC / self-consumption compliance;
- customer-as-final-consumer treatment inside a supplier-contract relationship;
- fixed-service-fee enforceability for a repeatable customer contract;
- consumer-credit-clean recurring-payment structure.

Please do not treat this memo as a request to approve the prospective WeRa business model. That is Track B.

## 2A. Entity Status Context

Please do not assume that a fully registered WeRa operating company already exists.

- WeRa Global is currently pre-registration; Portuguese registration is a Q2 2026 target.
- Current Torres Vedras arrangements should be read as founder-level / pre-formation arrangements unless evidence proves otherwise.
- No selected corporate structure is assumed for this memo; `PT CIC`, `NL STAK`, project SPV and ordinary PT operating company remain open or specialist-counsel hypotheses.
- Counsel should state who can sign a memorialisation now, and whether a future WeRa entity can later ratify or replace that pre-formation position.

## 4. Missing Evidence Carriers

Before this pilot can be used externally as commercial proof, the following are missing:

- signed written agreement or memorialisation;
- payment trail for components / installer / logistics;
- ownership / control / removal memo;
- grid-connection / supplier-contract / UPAC documentation, if any exists;
- telemetry export and proxy-savings sheet;
- maintenance ledger;
- site-verified as-built inventory;
- liability / insurance allocation.

## 5. Questions For Counsel

If time is limited, please answer these Priority 1 questions first:

- `P1`: What is the current legal character of the Torres Vedras arrangement?
- `P1`: What minimum written carrier can be signed now without misrepresenting the past?
- `P1`: Who must sign that carrier given the pre-registration / founder-level status?
- `P1`: How should title, operational control, access and removal rights be separated?
- `P1`: Does the verbally reported future buy-back understanding create any present legal obligation or expectation that should be memorialised now?
- `P1`: Please confirm that this pilot should not be described as proof of grid-connected UPAC / final-consumer / fixed-service-fee Track B unless additional grid and contract evidence exists.

### 5.1 Legal Characterisation

1. Based on the current facts, how would you characterise the Torres Vedras arrangement today?
2. Is it closer to:
   - informal installation support;
   - client-funded installation with founder-side operational management;
   - mandate / agency;
   - loan / use arrangement;
   - donation / contribution;
   - informal lease;
   - service arrangement;
   - another legal form?
3. Does the reported client-funded CapEX create a presumption of customer ownership of equipment?
4. If founder-side operational control exists, how should it be described without implying legal title?
5. Does the reported personal relationship between beneficiary and founder reduce or increase any legal/commercial risk?

### 5.2 Written Carrier To Sign Now

6. What is the minimum written document we should sign now to memorialise the arrangement truthfully?
7. Should this be:
   - signed memorandum of facts;
   - service / operation agreement;
   - site-access and maintenance consent;
   - equipment ownership / control memo;
   - retroactive agreement;
   - another document?
8. Can a document be signed now with retroactive effect, or should it only state historical facts and future operating terms?
9. Who must sign: site owner `D.N.`, beneficiary/user, Misha, future WeRa entity, or another party?
10. If WeRa is not yet fully registered or not yet the contracting entity, how should the founder / pre-formation role be written?

### 5.3 Ownership, Control and Removal

11. How should the document separate:
   - payer;
   - legal title holder;
   - beneficial owner;
   - operational manager;
   - maintenance party;
   - de-installation rights holder?
12. Who can remove equipment from the site?
13. What should happen if the site owner withdraws permission?
14. What happens if the beneficiary and site owner are not the same person?

### 5.4 CapEX, Buy-Back and Future Conversion

15. How should the reported client-funded CapEX be documented?
16. Does the possible future buy-back by WeRa create any current legal obligation?
17. Does the verbally reported future buy-back understanding create any present legal obligation or expectation that Miguel would need to memorialise now?
18. Should the current memorialisation avoid promising or defining that future buy-back path until Track B / specialist counsel answers financing, credit, lease or securities qualification?

### 5.5 Liability, Insurance and Maintenance

19. Who is liable today for:
   - fire;
   - roof / property damage;
   - third-party damage;
   - storm / force majeure;
   - battery / inverter failure?
20. What insurance should exist now?
21. How should maintenance obligations be memorialised?
22. Is weekly digital maintenance enough to create a formal operator duty?

### 5.6 Tax / Invoicing / Evidence

23. Does the lack of invoice / payment trail create tax or accounting issues?
24. Should the payment trail be collected before signing the memorialisation?
25. Are there VAT / invoice implications if the founder or future WeRa entity later buys back the equipment?

## 6. Requested Output From Counsel

Please provide:

1. A short legal characterisation of the current Torres Vedras arrangement.
2. A list of material risks in the current verbal / informal state.
3. A recommended minimum written carrier to sign now.
4. The parties who must sign it.
5. A bullet list of clauses that must be included.
6. A list of evidence you need before finalising the view.
7. A warning list of words or labels we should avoid when describing the pilot.
8. If Track A and Track B answers imply incompatible legal forms for the Torres Vedras object, please flag this explicitly and propose a compatible memorialisation path.
