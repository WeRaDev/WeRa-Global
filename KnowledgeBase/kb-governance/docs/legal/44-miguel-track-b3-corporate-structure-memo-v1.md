# Miguel Counsel Memo — Track B.3: Corporate, Securities and Tax Structure

> This memo is about **entity structure, securities, fund-regulation and tax**. It is not the energy-law contract package memo and not the Torres Vedras retrospective memo.

## 1. Core Ask

Please help us identify the minimum legally proportionate company / SPV / tax structure for WeRa's first repeatable contracts, and determine whether founder hypotheses such as `NL STAK -> PT CIC`, customer participation and tokenised real-estate payment should be treated as immediate structure or later specialist tracks.

The operating baseline to support is:

> customer remains final consumer; WeRa acts as third-party solar operator / service-layer holder; solar asset title preferably sits with customer or project SPV; customer pays a fixed service fee; no disguised `€/kWh` electricity resale.

This memo is the corporate / securities / tax route. It should not be used to settle energy-law or consumer-credit questions except to flag them back to Track B.1/B.2. If an authority question is needed, counsel should identify whether the route is `CMVM`, tax authority, another Portuguese body, or no authority contact at this stage.

## 2. Entity Status Context

Please do not assume that a fully registered WeRa operating company already exists.

- WeRa Global is currently pre-registration; Portuguese registration is a Q2 2026 target.
- Current arrangements and pilot work are founder-level / pre-formation unless a document proves otherwise.
- No selected corporate structure is assumed for this memo.
- `PT CIC`, `NL STAK`, ordinary PT operating company, project SPV and holding-company structures are options to test, not givens.

## 3. Status Map

| Item | Current status | Counsel question |
|---|---|---|

## 3A. Authority and Terminology Discipline

Please separate:

- ordinary first operating company;
- project SPV for one installation or a pool;
- PT `CIC` / `OIC` / collective investment structure, if any;
- NL STAK / foundation layer;
- customer participation, tokens, revenue rights or depositary receipts.

Historical project notes refer to `CVM`. Please confirm whether the intended Portuguese securities / markets authority is `CMVM`, whether another authority was meant, and whether any historical oral meeting can be relied on without written evidence. Current working rule: do not use `CIC`, STAK or customer-participation as first-contract baseline until this track is answered.

## 4. Questions For Counsel

### 4.1 Minimum First Structure

1. What is the minimum company structure needed to sign the first clean Portuguese customer contracts?
2. Can an ordinary PT operating company sign the first contracts and later migrate to a more complex holding / SPV / fund structure?
3. What are the costs, tax consequences and legal frictions of starting simple and restructuring later?
4. What pre-incorporation obligations can founders validly undertake, and how can the future company ratify or replace them?
5. Who should sign first contracts if WeRa is not yet incorporated?

### 4.2 Project SPVs and Asset Pools

6. When should a project SPV be introduced: first contract, first 10 installs, first debt facility, first investor, or later?
7. Can a project SPV hold one installation or a pool of installations?
8. Who contracts with the customer: operating company, SPV, or both?
9. Who contracts with installer and insurer?
10. What governance, accounting, VAT and collateral issues arise if assets sit in SPVs?

### 4.3 PT CIC Hypothesis

Founder hypothesis:

> A Portuguese collective investment company / `CIC` may support many small solar assets, reinvestment and later participant logic.

Questions:

11. What exact Portuguese legal structure is meant by "CIC" in this context?
12. Is it legally available and proportionate for this stage?
13. Can it hold or finance many small solar assets?
14. Does it trigger fund regulation, securities offering rules, licensing, depositary, management-company or authority approval requirements?
14A. Which authority route, if any, would apply: `CMVM`, tax authority, another body, or none before first contracts?
15. Can operating revenue be reinvested inside this structure as founder expects?
16. What tax/VAT/stamp-duty treatment would apply?

### 4.4 NL STAK -> PT Vehicle Hypothesis

Founder hypothesis:

> NL STAK first, then PT vehicle, may avoid a later additional bureaucratic session when shares are transferred to a Dutch administrative foundation for depositary receipts / tokenisation logic.

Questions:

17. What is the concrete cost/time difference between `STAK-first -> PT vehicle` and `PT-first -> later transfer to NL foundation / STAK`?
18. Would later ownership transfer to an NL foundation / STAK trigger additional Portuguese approvals, taxes, notary steps, bank/KYC steps or registry filings?
19. Does STAK-first help the first 1-10 customer contracts, or only later investor / tokenisation architecture?
20. Does STAK-first introduce bank account, tax residence, governance, KYC or fundraising complications?
21. What evidence is needed to compare these routes properly?

### 4.5 Customer Participation / Securities

22. Can customers later become economic participants without turning the customer contract into a securities offering or collective investment product?
23. If customers receive shares, tokens, revenue rights, depositary receipts or SPV interests, what licences, disclosures and approvals are required?
24. Can a customer payment ever convert into equity or investment participation without consumer-credit / securities issues?
25. Should customer participation be entirely excluded from first customer contracts?

### 4.6 Tokenised Real Estate / Crisis Payment

Founder far-path hypothesis:

> If a customer cannot pay, they could transfer a tokenised interest in the real estate where the installation is located, potentially giving WeRa priority buyout rights in a crisis/liquidation scenario.

Questions:

26. Please confirm whether this is outside the current operating baseline.
27. Which counsel specialisms are needed before this can be discussed seriously: real estate, securities, consumer protection, insolvency, tax, digital assets?
28. Could this be treated as secured lending, distressed acquisition, unfair-practice risk or securities issuance?
29. Should this be excluded from all customer-facing, authority-facing and investor materials until separately reviewed?

### 4.7 Tax / VAT Treatment

30. What tax/VAT treatment applies to fixed service fee revenue?
31. How does tax/VAT treatment change under a true lease alternative?
32. How does tax/VAT treatment change if excess generation revenue is captured or managed?
33. How do digital add-ons, telemetry, cloud/data-room services and partner services affect VAT classification?
34. Are there withholding, stamp-duty, permanent establishment, transfer-pricing or cross-border issues if NL STAK / PT vehicle is used?

## 5. Requested Output From Counsel

Please provide:

1. A recommended minimum first-stage entity path for the first clean contracts.
2. A red/yellow/green assessment of ordinary PT opco, project SPV, PT CIC and NL STAK-first routes.
3. A clear answer on whether STAK/CIC should be first baseline or later structure.
4. A confirmation of the correct authority naming and route, including whether historical `CVM` references should be corrected to `CMVM`.
5. A list of securities / fund-regulation triggers to avoid.
6. A tax/VAT issue list for service fee, lease alternative, excess generation and add-ons.
7. A recommendation on what not to mention in customer/authority-facing materials.
8. A list of questions that require energy-law or consumer-credit specialist counsel.
9. If Track A and Track B answers imply incompatible legal forms for the Torres Vedras object, please flag this explicitly and propose a compatible memorialisation path.
