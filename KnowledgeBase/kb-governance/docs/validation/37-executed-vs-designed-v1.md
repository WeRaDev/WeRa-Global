# WeRa — Executed vs Designed

> Этот файл нужен как противоядие против самой частой ошибки pre-seed-документов: когда planned object читается как already executed business.

## 1. Executed carriers

| Item | What is actually executed | What it proves | What it does not yet prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| Torres Vedras pilot | real installed site assembled by founder; founder reports client-funded CapEx with conditional future buy-back path; founder reports verbal agreement and ability to sign written carrier; current package reads as off-grid / no supplier-contract baseline | technical/operator familiarity, installation reality, existence of a live object, founder-reported evidence that a client accepted real economic participation, reported maintenance check after storms (30 January) | repeatable commercial baseline, clean contract package, recurring-payment economics, bill-vs-bill savings proof, grid-connected UPAC compliance, customer-as-final-consumer treatment in ordinary grid-supply context, executed written agreement trail |
| Iberia Renew Engineering | signed installer partner | delivery-side relationship exists, installation channel is not purely hypothetical | repeatable leasing channel, volume throughput, standardized deal flow |
| One client in proposal stage | live demand exists beyond the pilot | sales motion is not purely imagined | conversion, payment behaviour, contract quality |
| Sintra installer signal | installer-side demand for concierge/leasing-like help exists | partner channel may be real | signed distribution channel or repeatable originations |
| DT consultancy launch carrier | first external video launch + scheduling CTA published; launch evidence pack created | GTM channel is live, offer is externally reachable, top-of-funnel capture started | repeatable paid demand, session quality outcomes, stable conversion economics |
| Evidence brief v1 (AI platform claims) | formal claim-status + source-hygiene rules documented | external language can be constrained to evidence-safe facts | intent claims and enterprise-level causal claims are still not proven as facts |

## 3. Why Torres Vedras is load-bearing

Torres Vedras сейчас является не “одним из примеров”, а **единственным executed carrier solar operating object**.

Это означает:

- любой текст, который делает вид, что solar baseline уже коммерчески доказан, опирается на carrier, который пока не собран полностью;
- без pilot fact sheet Torres Vedras остаётся `technical pilot with commercial ambiguity`, а не `canonical commercial proof`;
- поэтому пилотный пакет — это не nice-to-have, а центральный reality task.

## 4. Minimum packages required next

### 4.1 Torres Vedras fact sheet

После заполнения questionnaire (`2026-04-23`) уже есть working-version reading:

- founder-reported client-funded CapEx;
- founder-reported verbal agreement, written carrier not yet executed;
- no confirmed recurring-payment trail;
- no bill-vs-bill baseline;
- no evidence that this site carries the ordinary grid-connected UPAC / final-consumer wrapper;
- pilot status = `customer installation with incomplete commercial wrapper`.

Теперь нужно превратить эту working-version в external-grade evidence pack:

- подписать written agreement или signed memorialization;
- приложить payment trail;
- собрать ownership/control/removal memo;
- выгрузить telemetry и proxy-savings sheet;
- оформить maintenance ledger.

Even after this fact sheet is collected, Torres Vedras should still be treated as Track A evidence unless it can also show grid-connected UPAC / supply-contract facts. It should not be upgraded into proof of the prospective Track B baseline by memorialisation alone.

### 4.2 First repeatable contract pack

Нужно собрать:

- service agreement summary;
- site-access and maintenance terms;
- billing logic;
- default / cure / termination logic;
- insurance allocation.

### 4.3 First reality-grade economics pack

Нужно иметь:

- edge-only BOM recut;
- actual proposal economics for selected subset;
- base case without WiFi Map;
- explicit gap between unit economics and company burn.

### 4.4 DT consultancy proof pack

Для перевода DT-launch из статуса `executed channel` в `executed business line` нужно собрать:

- completed session ledger (минимум 10 сессий с унифицированным outcome-note форматом);
- conversion chain (`impressions -> clicks -> bookings -> completed -> paid -> upgrade`);
- 3 case write-ups с ограниченными и верифицируемыми KPI-формулировками;
- consent/privacy compliance пакет для записи и транскрипции.

## 5. Working rule

Пока элемент не имеет executed carrier, его нельзя подавать как “уже доказанную реальность”. Его можно подавать только как:

- selected baseline,
- designed model,
- or open legal hypothesis.
