# Grant and Farmer-Support Funding Plan

## 1. Purpose

This note turns the May 2026 funding scan into an action plan for WeRa.

It separates three different uses of public funding:

1. **Customer-side subsidies**: grants that farmers, agricultural producers, regantes associations or SME clients can use to buy energy-efficiency / PV / storage solutions.
2. **WeRa-side company grants**: grants that WeRa could apply to directly as an SME / clean-tech operator.
3. **Consortium / policy grants**: EU or Portugal 2030 routes that require municipalities, public entities, research partners or multi-party consortia.

Current working conclusion: the fastest actionable route is not a grant for WeRa itself, but a **grant-enabled customer acquisition package for farmers and agricultural users**, starting with IFAP / Fundo Ambiental AAC 02/2026.

| P1 | COMPETE 2030 / SITCE MPR-2026-01 - decarbonisation and energy efficiency | Open, phase 2 deadline 2026-05-29; RCI until 2026-12-30 | Companies of any size | Use for SME / agro-SME energy-efficiency projects |

## 3. P1 Action Plan: Farmer Grant-Enabled Offer

### 3.1. IFAP / Fundo Ambiental AAC 02/2026

**Source status:** official IFAP notice, published 2026-04-30.

**What it supports:** equipment and infrastructure for energy efficiency, energy production and storage in the agricultural sector.

**Relevant eligible items:**

- energy audit and certification;
- photovoltaic panels for autonomous energy production, limited to self-consumption needs;
- batteries for energy autonomy;
- solar water heating systems;
- heat pumps / high-efficiency heating and cooling;
- LED lighting;
- thermal insulation;
- capacitor banks;
- variable-speed drives for pumps and motors.

**Eligible beneficiaries:**

- `Associacoes de Regantes`;
- dairy producers with REAP registration and dairy herd registered in SNIRA as of 2026-04-30;
- agricultural and agro-livestock producers with active 2025 commitments under the `Uso Eficiente da Agua` intervention.

**Funding shape:**

- total budget: EUR 15,000,000;
- sub-budgets: EUR 7.5M for irrigation associations, EUR 3.75M for dairy producers, EUR 3.75M for agricultural/agro-livestock producers;
- support form: non-repayable grant;
- reference support level: 80% of reference cost;
- limit: EUR 50,000 for agricultural beneficiaries; EUR 300,000 for other eligible types;
- submission window: 2026-05-27 10:00 to 2026-06-30 18:00, or earlier if budget is exhausted;
- admission is by order of submission against available budget.

**WeRa interpretation:** this is a direct commercial lever. WeRa should not treat this as a company grant. The immediate offer should be: "we help eligible farms assemble a PV + storage + efficiency package that can be submitted under AAC 02/2026."

### 3.3. First outreach targets

Use the grant as a reason to speak to concrete buyers:

- dairy producers with high energy load;
- irrigation associations;
- farms already using pumps and motors where variable-speed drives can reduce consumption;
- agricultural producers already under `Uso Eficiente da Agua`;
- rural SME with agricultural processing, only if eligible through the agricultural route or through SITCE.

Avoid generic "solar for everyone" messaging. The IFAP call is administratively narrow.

## 4. Marketing Campaign Hypotheses

This campaign is a validation test, not a brand campaign. Current constraint: WeRa does **not** yet have a reliable partner-entry route through cooperatives, agricultural consultants or local associations. Therefore the first marketing test should validate whether direct paid acquisition can produce qualified farmer conversations fast enough before the AAC 02/2026 window closes.

### 4.1. Core campaign hypothesis

| ID | Hypothesis | Test | Success signal | Stop / revise signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MH1 | A grant-specific message will outperform a generic solar message for farmers | Meta ads / posts framed around IFAP AAC 02/2026 eligibility check | WhatsApp lead cost EUR 3-8 and at least 20 leads in 7 days | WhatsApp lead cost > EUR 15 or <10 leads in 7 days |
| MH2 | Farmers will respond better to WhatsApp-first contact than to website forms | Ads and posts send directly to WhatsApp; landing page is only proof page | >60% of inbound leads answer first screening questions | Leads click but do not answer screening questions |
| MH3 | Eligibility screening is the right first offer | First CTA: "verificacao gratuita de elegibilidade" | >30% of leads complete 5-question screening | Leads ask only for price and do not provide eligibility data |
| MH4 | The strongest qualifying segments are dairy producers, irrigation users and farms with high pump/motor load | Segment leads by farm type and electricity-use pattern | At least 3 qualified leads in one segment within 10 days | Lead quality is scattered and no segment emerges |
| MH5 | Direct acquisition can create partner intelligence even without partner-entry | Ask every lead who normally helps them with IFAP / candidaturas | At least 5 named accountants, technicians, associations or consultants | No repeated intermediary names emerge |

### 4.2. Test budget

Minimum test budget: **EUR 150-300 for 5-7 days**.

Do not scale spend until the campaign produces qualified leads, not just cheap clicks.

Working KPI ladder:

- 30-50 WhatsApp leads;
- 10-15 eligibility calls;
- 3-5 qualified farmer / agricultural leads;
- 1-2 near-term technical proposals;
- at least 5 names of real local intermediaries for future partner outreach.

### 4.3. Direct-response funnel

| Step | Mechanic | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meta ads / Facebook local posts in Portuguese | Awareness around short IFAP funding window |
| 2 | Click-to-WhatsApp | Avoid long forms and reduce friction |
| 3 | Five-question screening | Separate eligible farmers from generic solar interest |
| 4 | 15-minute call | Confirm eligibility, energy need and urgency |
| 5 | Request electricity bill / equipment photos | Move from interest to technical sizing |
| 6 | Mini technical note | Show PV / battery / efficiency option and grant-dependency |
| 7 | Proposal / installer visit | Convert only if eligibility and economics hold |

### 4.4. First Portuguese copy to test

**Ad / post variant A:**

> Apoio publico 2026 para exploracoes agricolas.  
> Paineis solares, baterias e eficiencia energetica podem ser elegiveis para apoio IFAP/Fundo Ambiental.  
> A WeRa faz uma verificacao preliminar gratuita da sua elegibilidade.  
> Envie mensagem no WhatsApp.

**Ad / post variant B:**

> Tem custos elevados de eletricidade na sua exploracao agricola?  
> Verifique se pode candidatar-se a apoio para autoconsumo solar, baterias ou equipamentos de eficiencia energetica.  
> Verificacao preliminar gratuita. A aprovacao depende das regras do aviso e da dotacao disponivel.

### 4.5. Screening questions

Use these questions in WhatsApp before scheduling a call:

1. Qual e o tipo de exploracao? leite / regadio / agricola / agropecuaria / outro.
2. Tem atividade registada no IFAP?
3. Tem fatura de eletricidade da exploracao?
4. Usa bombas, motores, frio ou outros equipamentos com consumo elevado?
5. Em que concelho fica a exploracao?

### 4.6. Legal-safe marketing boundaries

Allowed claims:

- WeRa helps with preliminary eligibility screening.
- WeRa helps prepare a technical package for PV, batteries and energy-efficiency measures.
- Approval depends on IFAP / Fundo Ambiental rules, available budget and authority decision.

Avoid:

- "grant guaranteed";
- "free solar panels";
- "80% guaranteed support";
- "government-backed WeRa";
- "zero upfront solar" until counsel confirms ownership / subsidy compatibility;
- claims that WeRa is an approved public intermediary unless formally confirmed.

Safest operating frame for this campaign: **customer-owned grant-funded equipment + WeRa as technical / service coordinator**, not WeRa-owned lease/service model, unless counsel confirms the latter is compatible with the grant rules.

## 5. P1 Action Plan: SME / Agro-SME Decarbonisation

### 5.1. COMPETE 2030 / SITCE MPR-2026-01

**Source status:** official COMPETE 2030 page.

**What it supports:** reduction of energy consumption and GHG emissions through low-carbon equipment, processes and technologies, with complementary incorporation of renewable energy.

**Beneficiaries:** companies of any size in eligible mainland regions.

**Timeline:**

- phase 2 for general regime: deadline 2026-05-29 at 18:00;
- phase 3 for RCI: deadline 2026-12-30 at 18:00.

**Funding shape:**

- FEDER indicative budget: EUR 165,000,000;
- maximum cofinancing under general regime: up to 85%, subject to aid intensity rules.

**WeRa interpretation:** this can support SME decarbonisation packages, but the 2026-05-29 deadline is too close for a cold application unless there is already a ready client. Treat it as:

- immediate check for any client already near-ready;
- otherwise, monitor for future phase / related calls;
- use as a template for grant-enabled SME offer.

## 6. P2 Routes to Monitor

### 6.1. Portugal 2030 / Alentejo 2030 - Autoconsumo and CER

Planned launch: May-August 2026.

Current known shape:

- region: Alentejo;
- promoter type: public;
- modality: co-promotion;
- operation: renewable energy communities (`Comunidades de Energia Renovavel`).

**WeRa interpretation:** not a direct sales grant to farmers. It is potentially useful if WeRa can partner with a municipality, cooperative, public entity or local development association around a rural energy-community project.

Action:

- identify 2-3 Alentejo public / cooperative partners;
- prepare a CER concept note only after legal routing confirms what WeRa can safely claim as operator/service holder.

### 6.2. Portugal 2030 / STEP Inovacao Produtiva Energia

Planned launch: May-August 2026.

Current known shape:

- promoter type: private;
- regions: Norte, Centro, Alentejo;
- programme: COMPETE 2030;
- focus: clean technologies / productive investment.

**WeRa interpretation:** possible WeRa-side company route, but only if the project is framed as a concrete productive investment or technology-enabled clean-energy service. It is not a generic startup grant.

Action:

- wait for formal notice;
- prepare a 1-page technology boundary: what is actually proprietary or operationally differentiated in SolarSeed;
- do not rely on tokenisation, GPU hosting, or WERA Cloud as proof of current technological maturity.

## 7. P3 EU Consortium Routes

### 7.1. LIFE 2026 Clean Energy Transition

Deadline: 2026-09-16.

Relevant topics include:

- project development assistance for sustainable energy investments;
- cooperation among energy communities;
- one-stop-shops and integrated clean energy services;
- digital / clean energy transition market activation.

**WeRa interpretation:** potentially useful for a consortium around rural energy communities or a local one-stop-shop for small self-consumption projects. Not suitable as a quick customer subsidy.

Action:

- only pursue if there is a credible public / NGO / municipal lead;
- do not let LIFE distract from Track B legal and first customer work.

### 7.2. EIC Accelerator 2026

Relevant challenge themes include:

- advanced materials for renewable energy and energy storage;
- biotech for regenerating agricultural soils;
- deep tech for climate adaptation.

**WeRa interpretation:** weak fit for the current service/operator model. Fit improves only if WeRa can demonstrate defensible technology, not just a combined business model.

Action:

- keep on watchlist;
- revisit after SolarSeed has stronger technical evidence or a distinct product component.

## 9. Risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| WeRa sells before eligibility is confirmed | Farmer-facing grants have narrow criteria | Use screening checklist before proposal |
| Funding is exhausted early | IFAP call is first-come / budget-limited | Prepare before 2026-05-27 opening |
| WeRa becomes grant consultant instead of energy operator | Bad focus and low leverage | Keep grant support tied to SolarSeed package |
| Current legal model conflicts with grant ownership rules | Subsidised assets may have non-alienation / retention obligations | Check whether customer owns funded equipment before using service/operator structure |
| Claims exceed current evidence | Investor and grant language can drift into designed reality | Use `working` / `tested` / `confirmed` claim status |
| Paid ads create many unqualified farmers | No partner-entry route means lower trust and more noise | Treat campaign as a 7-day validation test; scale only after qualified-call conversion is proven |
| Direct messages create privacy / direct-marketing exposure | Cold electronic outreach to individuals can require consent | Use opt-in ad flows and inbound WhatsApp; do not scrape and mass-message farmers |
