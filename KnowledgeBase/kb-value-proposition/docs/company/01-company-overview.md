## The Analogy

WeRa has been described internally as:

> "EDP (Energias de Portugal) + Airbnb + cooperative on steroids"
> Also: "ADP with Airbnb run by a Starbucks model"

- **EDP** → energy generation and distribution at scale
- **Airbnb** → distributed infrastructure owned by participants
- **Cooperative on steroids** → tokenized governance with real profit-sharing
- **Starbucks model** → franchise-like scalability with consistent branding and experience

## Three Core Problems Solved

1. **Energy dependence** — households and SMEs locked into grid pricing and vulnerable to outages (e.g., April 2025 Iberian blackout)
2. **Data sovereignty** — individuals and organizations dependent on Big Tech (Google, Microsoft) for digital tools, with privacy erosion
3. **Investment exclusion** — ordinary citizens excluded from infrastructure investment returns; wealth concentration
