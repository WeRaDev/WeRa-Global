## STAK Mechanics

A Dutch STAK (Stichting Administratiekantoor) separates:
- **Voting rights** — retained by the STAK board (Legal Board)
- **Economic interest** — distributed to certificate holders (WeP token holders)

This enables:
- Token holders to receive dividends / profit-sharing without direct voting power
- The Legal Board to make governance decisions on behalf of all stakeholders
- Golden Share mechanism: Foundation can veto STAK decisions via the 12% governance instrument

Reference: [Share Council — Legal Structure of a STAK](https://sharecouncil.co/en/blog/legal-structure-of-a-stak)

## CELL Companies (Sub-Fund Structure)

WeRa Capital operates as a CIC (Sociedade de Investimento Coletivo) containing **CELL companies** — administration units that function as sub-funds / daughter-companies within the collective investment structure.

### Portuguese CIC / SIC Framework
- Governed by the **Asset Management Regime (RGA)**, approved by Decree-Law 27/2023 and implemented by CMVM Regulation 7/2023 (effective January 2024)
- CICs (Sociedade de Investimento Coletivo) have **legal personality** — they are autonomous entities, unlike contractual investment funds
- Can be **self-managed** or managed by a third-party fund manager (SGOIC — Sociedade Gestora de Organismos de Investimento Coletivo)
- **Sub-funds are legally recognized** under the AMR, with **segregation of assets** between sub-funds confirmed by law ([ICLG — Alternative Funds Portugal 2025](https://iclg.com/practice-areas/alternative-investment-funds-laws-and-regulations/portugal))
- Participants hold **shares** (ações), not units — they are shareholders in the company
- CMVM authorization required; large fund managers (AUM >€100M) need full authorization, small managers get simplified authorization
- Minimum initial capital: €125,000 + 0.02% of AUM above €250M (capped at €10M)

### CELL = Sub-Fund
- Each CELL holds a specific cohort of co-owned RWAs (SolarSeed installations in a geographic region or customer segment)
- Assets within each CELL are **legally segregated** from other CELLs
- A CELL acts as an administration unit / daughter-company within WeRa Capital
- When a customer enters mutual leasing (see `05-Tokenomics-and-Governance.md`), they become a **shareholder in the CELL** that holds the tokenized RWA
- CELLs can hold: real estate (tokenized via mutual leasing), SolarSeed equipment, leasing agreements, energy production rights
- Structure modeled on: "dedicated to operating cohorts of co-owned RWA — 'CELL' companies that hold shares of all RWA within the conglomerate across Europe" (structure.md)

### Digital Separation of Power
- The three-entity structure implements a "digital separation of power principle in arbitrage of sectoral balances" (structure.md)
- Modeled on high-level state government processes: public (Foundation) / private (Capital) / foreign (STAK) network model
- Each entity acts as a check on the others via the BFT governance cascade

Source: [RFF Lawyers — RE Investment Companies in Portugal](https://www.rfflawyers.com/en/know-how/newsletters/real-estate-investment-and-real-estate-investment-companies-in-portugal/5229/), [Chambers — Alternative Funds Portugal 2025](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/alternative-funds-2025/portugal)
