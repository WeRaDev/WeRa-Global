# Revenue Streams

## Purpose
Define Human Center's candidate pricing/revenue model.

## Scope
- In scope: market-benchmark-derived pricing ranges for the initial service menu.
- Out of scope: final, contracted pricing (none exists yet — no client contracts signed).

## Current State
Market-benchmark pricing (European agency comparables, solo-consultant buyer), from `HumanCenter.Pricing.md`:
- Email triage/drafting/follow-up automation: €1,500-€4,000 basic; €3,000-€7,000 advanced (context/attachments/integrations).
- Accounting/document automation: €2,500-€6,000 basic bookkeeping automation; €5,000-€12,000+ for more sophisticated tax + accounting automation.
- Combined accounting + email package: roughly €5,000-€9,000 (shared infra reduces marginal cost).
- Ongoing monitoring/support retainer: €100-€500/month normal, €500-€1,000/month high-touch.
- Recommended target for a first combined offer: ~€5,000-€8,000 setup + €150-€400/month retainer, with €4,000-€5,000 considered a good low quote and €6,000-€8,000 very normal for this scope.

## Decisions / Rules
- These figures are third-party market benchmarks, not Human Center's own validated pricing; do not quote them to a client as final pricing without a cost-model check (`../../../kb-cost-structure/docs/model/08-cost-structure.md`).
- Avoid PE-fund-scale pricing (tens of thousands of euros/dollars) since the buyer is a solo consultant/small firm, not the fund itself (see `../../../kb-customers/docs/gtm/03-customer-segments.md`).
