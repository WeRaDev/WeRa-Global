# Revenue Streams

## Purpose
Define Human Center's revenue model, and clearly separate two distinct pricing threads found in research that must not be conflated.

## Scope
- In scope: Product 1 ("Shrinking AI") session-flow revenue structure, and the separate market-benchmark pricing thread for a possible future implementation-support/automation-agency tail.
- Out of scope: final, contracted pricing (none exists yet for either thread — no client contracts signed, and no documented price for the core paid session).

## Current State
**Critical gap: Shrinking AI's core paid-session price is undocumented anywhere in current research.** The session flow (see `../../../kb-key-activities/docs/execution/04-key-activities.md`) is: (1) free 15-minute empathy call, (2) a paid, structured RCGFC session producing an AI-ready problem statement and instruction set, (3) an optional retainer/implementation-support follow-on. Steps 1 and 2 are validated as the core product; no source document states what the paid session in step 2 actually costs.

There are two separate, non-equivalent pricing threads in the research corpus. They must not be merged into one number:
1. **Shrinking AI session pricing** — the actual product's pricing. Status: **not documented anywhere**. This is the priority pricing gap.
2. **`HumanCenter.Pricing.md` automation-agency benchmarks** — market-benchmark pricing for building/operating automation (email triage, bookkeeping/document automation), sourced from named European/US agency comparables (ABC OPTIM, FETCHER Solutions, NeuraWeb, Flow Architects, Bitech Prime, Go Rogue Ops, Connex Digital):
   - Email triage/drafting/follow-up automation: €1,500-€4,000 basic; €3,000-€7,000 advanced.
   - Accounting/document automation: €2,500-€6,000 basic; €5,000-€12,000+ advanced.
   - Combined accounting + email package: roughly €5,000-€9,000.
   - Ongoing monitoring/support retainer: €100-€500/month normal, €500-€1,000/month high-touch.
   - This thread describes a *build-and-operate automation agency* business model, which is not what the validated session-flow research describes. It is relevant only as a possible price anchor for the optional "implementation support" follow-on after a Shrinking AI session (or conceivably for the founder's own back-office use) — it is not evidence for what the core session should cost.

## Decisions / Rules
- Do not use the `HumanCenter.Pricing.md` figures as Shrinking AI's session price in any client-facing material; they describe a different (automation-build) offer shape.
- Do not quote either thread's figures as final pricing without a cost-model check (`../../../kb-cost-structure/docs/model/08-cost-structure.md`).
- Avoid PE-fund-scale pricing (tens of thousands of euros/dollars) since the buyer is a solo consultant/small firm, not a fund (see `../../../kb-customers/docs/gtm/03-customer-segments.md`).
