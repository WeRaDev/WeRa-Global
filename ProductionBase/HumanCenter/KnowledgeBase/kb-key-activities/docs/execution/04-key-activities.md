# Key Activities

## Purpose
List Human Center's core delivery activities for Product 1 ("Shrinking AI").

## Scope
- In scope: activities required to run the empathy-call-to-deliverable session flow.
- Out of scope: Product 2 ("Career Development") activities; built-automation delivery (a separate, optional follow-on, not the core activity).

## Current State
- **Lead qualification**: a short pre-booking form (2-3 questions) to qualify fit before a call is booked, reducing wasted founder time on unqualified calls.
- **Free 15-minute empathy call**: low-pressure discovery conversation; explicit goal is trust-building, not selling.
- **Paid structured session facilitation**: guiding the client through the RCGFC (Role, Context, Goal, Format, Constraints) framework to produce their problem statement and instruction set.
- **2-hour deliverable production**: turning the session output into the promised written deliverable within the committed turnaround.
- **Optional retainer/implementation support**: a follow-on activity for clients who want ongoing help, scoped separately from the core session.
- **Proof-asset creation**: producing case write-ups and consent-evidence documentation from completed sessions (currently a named gap, see `../../../kb-metrics/docs/financial/09-key-metrics.md`).
- **Market/competitive research maintenance**: desk research and pricing-benchmark refresh cadence.

## Decisions / Rules
- Lead qualification happens before scheduling, not during the free call, to protect founder bandwidth.
- Every session output must remain a bounded, written artifact; do not let sessions drift into open-ended consulting without a separate retainer scope.
