# Key Partners

## Purpose
Identify candidate partnerships for Human Center.

## Scope
- In scope: partnership hypotheses only; no partnerships are signed yet.
- Out of scope: confirmed/contracted partnerships (none exist).

## Current State
- No partnerships are currently signed.
- **For Shrinking AI (Product 1) — primary candidate: a consultant/freelancer referral network.** Because the primary ICP (A4 — AI-frustrated consultants/freelancers, see `../../../kb-customers/docs/gtm/03-customer-segments.md`) is itself a distribution-capable segment, early clients are natural referral partners. This is explicitly noted in internal planning as a "consultant marketplace" ambition, though not yet built or tested.
- **For Automation Center (Product 3) — candidates promoted from hypothesis to directly relevant, now that Automation Center is a real product**: accounting firms/bookkeepers (natural handoff point and referral source for bookkeeping-automation clients), the n8n/Make/Zapier implementation-partner ecosystem (subcontracting or co-delivery for larger builds), OCR/document-processing vendors, and accounting-software integration partners (e.g. Xero/QuickBooks-style ecosystems) for combined packages.
- Other candidates (unvalidated): sibling WeRa Global projects with relevant EU-hosting/infrastructure experience (e.g. `ProductionBase/FilantropiaSolar`).

## Decisions / Rules
- Prioritize the consultant-referral hypothesis for Shrinking AI, since it is directly supported by validated ICP research.
- For Automation Center, prioritize accounting-firm/bookkeeper referral partnerships and accounting-software integration partners, since they map directly to the documented service scope (see `../../../kb-value-proposition/docs/products/03-automation-center.md`); treat the automation-tooling ecosystem as a delivery-capacity partner, not primarily a lead-gen channel.
