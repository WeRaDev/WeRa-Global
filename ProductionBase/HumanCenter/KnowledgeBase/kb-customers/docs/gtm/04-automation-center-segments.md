# Automation Center Customer Segments

## Purpose
Define Product 3 ("Automation Center")'s target customer base, distinct from Shrinking AI's scored ICP.

## Scope
- In scope: the broad professional-services/solo-consultant target, and the financial-advisory sub-segment named in the discovery-interview research.
- Out of scope: Shrinking AI's scored A1-A4 segments (`03-customer-segments.md`); Product 2 segments.

## Current State
Automation Center's addressable market is deliberately **broader** than Shrinking AI's: any professional-services firm or solo consultant needing back-office automation, not limited to the AI-frustrated-consultant persona. Two evidenced sub-segments:

1. **Solo consultant/independent professional with confidential client data** (the original pricing-research persona: an independent consultant working with investment funds in Europe). Needs email and accounting/bookkeeping automation with a human-approval, audit-logged, DPA-backed security posture because of confidential deal/investor information. Buyer is the solo consultant, not the fund itself — do not price or position as a "PE AI solution."
2. **Small financial-advisory or professional-services firms with distinct roles** (the discovery-interview research persona): firms large enough to have separate adviser/owner, operational colleague, and compliance/data-owner functions. These firms may fall under sector-specific supervision, communications and recordkeeping obligations (e.g. FINRA-adjacent duties in relevant US-perimeter firms), which must be established per client rather than assumed.

Unlike Shrinking AI, **no weighted ICP scoring model exists yet** for Automation Center's audience — this is a hypothesis-stage target definition pending real client interviews.

## Decisions / Rules
- Do not import Shrinking AI's A1-A4 scoring model uncritically; Automation Center's buyer is not necessarily the same "AI-frustrated consultant" persona and may be a larger, more process-mature firm.
- Do not price or position Automation Center as an enterprise/PE-fund-facing AI vendor; the buyer remains a solo consultant or small firm even when their own clients are funds.
- For any prospect whose data includes regulated financial-advisory activity, run the compliance/data-owner discovery route before proposing a pilot (see `../../../kb-solution/docs/strategy/03-automation-center-solution.md`).
