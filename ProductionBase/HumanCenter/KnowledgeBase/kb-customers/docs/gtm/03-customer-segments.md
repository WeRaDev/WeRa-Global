# Customer Segments

## Purpose
Define Human Center's initial target customer segment(s) and go-to-market approach.

## Scope
- In scope: beachhead segment definition and broader addressable segment.
- Out of scope: enterprise/PE-fund direct clients (see Decisions / Rules).

## Current State
- Beachhead segment (from pricing research): solo/independent consultants and small professional-services firms with a bounded back-office workload (one mailbox, one accounting stack), including those whose own clients are investment funds.
- Broader segment: SMEs wanting bounded, high-trust automations rather than open-ended AI transformation programs.

## Decisions / Rules
- Do not position or price Human Center as a PE-fund-facing AI vendor; the buyer is the solo consultant/small firm, not the fund itself.
- Any client whose data includes confidential deal/investor information requires the stricter security posture (human approval, audit logs, DPA, EU data residency) per `../../../../SOUL.md`.
