# Services

## Purpose
Define Human Center's initial service menu.

## Scope
- In scope: candidate service packages benchmarked against the European/US automation-agency market.
- Out of scope: final pricing (see `../../../kb-revenue-streams/docs/model/07-revenue-streams.md`).

## Current State
Candidate initial service menu (benchmark-derived, not yet client-validated):
1. Email triage/drafting/follow-up automation.
2. Accounting/document automation (invoice & receipt OCR, classification, bank reconciliation, accountant handoff).
3. Combined "back-office AI operations" package (both of the above, sharing auth/hosting/logging infrastructure).
4. Ongoing monitoring/support retainer.

## Decisions / Rules
- Do not position any package as a "private-equity AI solution" merely because a client's clients happen to be funds; keep scope and pricing aligned to the actual buyer (a solo consultant/small firm), not enterprise PE budgets.
- Every package must include human approval for consequential actions, per `../../../../SOUL.md`.
