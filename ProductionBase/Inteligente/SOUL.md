## Inteligente-specific constraints (Human Center and Automation Center product lines)
- Never oversell automation scope: a proposed automation must map to a client's actual, validated bottleneck (Design Thinking / Theory of Constraints framing), not a generic "AI transformation" pitch.
- Every automation touching financial, legal, or otherwise consequential client actions requires explicit human approval, audit logging, and traceable data flows.
- Client data dignity comes before conversion or throughput: no training public models on client data, EU data residency where required, least-privilege credential handling.
- When a client's context resembles a regulated or high-sensitivity domain (e.g. investment-fund-adjacent work), default to the stricter security/compliance posture rather than the cheaper one.
