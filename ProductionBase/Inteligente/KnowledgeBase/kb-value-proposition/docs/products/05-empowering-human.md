# Empowering Human

## Purpose
Define Empowering Human, the Human Center product line's second service: discovery of the most suitable AI applications for business and personal use.

## Scope
- In scope: the hypothesis-stage value proposition for Empowering Human, including how it folds in the earlier, unresearched "Career Development" B2C concept.
- Out of scope: Shrinking AI (Human Center's other, validated service, see `02-services.md`); the Automation Center product line and its Financial Automation service (see `03-automation-center.md`, `04-financial-automation.md`); any claims presented as validated rather than hypothesis.

## Current State
- **Positioning**: Empowering Human helps an individual or organization discover which AI applications are actually suitable for their specific business or personal goals, as distinct from Shrinking AI's focus on converting an existing AI frustration into a bounded, AI-ready problem statement. Where Shrinking AI assumes the client already has a specific pain point in mind, Empowering Human starts earlier in the journey: "which AI tools/approaches are even relevant to me?"
- **Relationship to former "Career Development" concept**: an earlier, unresearched B2C product idea ("Career Development") is folded into Empowering Human's personal-use angle (e.g. an individual exploring AI tools for career growth or personal productivity) rather than existing as its own separate product line.
- **Audience**: both B2B (an organization wanting a suitability assessment before adopting AI tools) and B2C (an individual seeking personal guidance) angles are plausible; neither has been researched or scored yet.
- **Stage**: no desk research, no pricing, no delivery methodology, and no proof assets exist for this service. This document exists to reserve the service's place in the knowledgebase structure, not to assert validated claims.
