# Expanding Human

## Purpose
Define Expanding Human, the Human Center service for exploring where AI may augment a person's or organization's goals, problems, and ambitions.

## Scope
- In scope: the current hypothesis-stage positioning, need-based routing, and one founder-reported paid delivery observation.
- Out of scope: Shrinking AI's work with people already using AI without satisfactory results (`02-services.md`); Automation Center's implementation of organizational workflow automation (`03-automation-center.md`); claims that the audience, price, or delivery method is validated.

## Current State
- **Positioning**: Expanding Human helps individuals and companies explore how AI might support their goals, solve problems, or advance ambitions when the relevant opportunity is not yet clear. This differs from Shrinking AI, which addresses unsatisfactory results from AI the client is already using, and Automation Center, which implements a sufficiently defined organizational process-automation need.
- **Contextual need**: the founder reports anxiety and confusion arising from the AI information environment as context for this service. This is a founder-reported observation, not evidence of prevalence or a validated market problem.
- **One paid delivery observation (2026-09-29)**: Client A, a self-publishing writer, separately paid €50 for a 60-minute follow-up consultancy asking how AI could be used in the business beyond editing and formatting. The consultant delivered a business model canvas and a "marketing scope of AI application." This is one founder-reported engagement, not a stable price, validated willingness-to-pay estimate, general ICP, or repeatable delivery method. See `02-services.md` and `../../../kb-revenue-streams/docs/model/07-revenue-streams.md`.
- **Maturity**: no dedicated market research, scored segments, standardized delivery method, or repeatability evidence has been established. The earlier "Career Development" concept is not a separate service; no standalone career-coaching offer is defined here.

## Decisions / Rules
- Keep the audience and delivery model at `evidence_status: hypothesis`; one paid engagement does not validate a general B2B/B2C segment, ICP, method, or price.
- Route by the client's need, not by a fixed product-line-first sales sequence: unclear AI opportunities go to Expanding Human; ineffective results from AI already in use go to Shrinking AI; a defined organizational process-automation requirement goes to Automation Center.
- Keep the service distinct from Shrinking AI and Automation Center in positioning and delivery claims. A referral between product lines is appropriate when the client's need crosses their boundary; it is not evidence of conversion.
- Do not represent €50/60 minutes as a standard Expanding Human rate. It is the fee and duration of one founder-reported session only.

## Evidence

Client artifacts, independent confirmation, repeatability, client outcome, and consultant effort were not reviewed.
- **Founder-defined Human Center positioning and need-based routing (2026-09-29)** — primary statement of service intent; not market validation.
