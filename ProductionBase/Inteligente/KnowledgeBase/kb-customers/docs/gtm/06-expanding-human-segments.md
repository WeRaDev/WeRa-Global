# Expanding Human — Customer Segments (Hypothesis)

## Purpose
Record the broad, hypothesis-stage audience for Expanding Human and separate it from the one reported paid engagement.

## Scope
- In scope: individuals and organizations exploring how AI could support their goals, problems, or ambitions when the relevant opportunity is unclear.
- Out of scope: Shrinking AI's scored segments (`03-customer-segments.md`); Automation Center and Financial Automation segments (`04-automation-center-segments.md`, `05-financial-automation-segments.md`); any unvalidated ICP scoring or audience ranking.

## Current State
- **Broad need hypothesis**: individuals and companies may seek help identifying where AI could usefully augment their work or ambitions. The relative priority of B2C and B2B audiences, and any narrower segment, has not been established.
- **Contextual need**: founder-reported anxiety or confusion about the AI information environment may shape this need. It is not yet a validated segment attribute or estimate of market prevalence.
- **One observed engagement**: a self-publishing writer paid for a 60-minute Expanding Human follow-up about using AI in the business beyond editing and formatting. This single case does not establish an ICP, recurring demand, or a segment ranking.
- No segment-scoring model, dedicated customer research, or repeatable acquisition channel exists for this service.

## Decisions / Rules
- Keep all segment definitions at `evidence_status: hypothesis`; do not present the broad audience or the single writer case as a validated ICP.
- Do not infer that Expanding Human is exclusively B2C from the one writer engagement; the founder-defined service includes both individuals and companies, with audience priority still open.
- Route a person or organization to Expanding Human when its AI opportunity is unclear; route existing but ineffective AI use to Shrinking AI and defined organizational workflow automation to Automation Center.

## Evidence
- **Founder-defined Human Center positioning (2026-09-29)** — primary statement that Expanding Human serves individuals and companies exploring AI support for goals, problems, or ambitions; not independent segment validation.
- **Founder-reported Expanding Human engagement (business-analysis session, 2026-09-29)** — one paid observation involving a self-publishing writer; no client confirmation, artifacts, or repeated demand reviewed.
