# Automation Center Customer Segments

## Purpose
Define Automation Center's target customer base at the product-line level, distinct from Human Center's scored ICPs.

## Scope
- In scope: the broad professional-services/solo-consultant target for Automation Center as a whole.
- Out of scope: Human Center's scored Shrinking AI segments (`03-customer-segments.md`) and hypothesis-stage Empowering Human segments (`06-empowering-human-segments.md`); Financial Automation's specific client profiles, see `05-financial-automation-segments.md`.

## Current State
Automation Center's addressable market is deliberately **broader** than Human Center's: any professional-services firm or solo consultant needing back-office automation, not limited to a single persona. The line's generic target is a solo consultant or small professional-services firm seeking email, accounting/bookkeeping, or document-automation support without an in-house automation team.

Financial Automation, the line's first named service, narrows this general target to financial-industry clients with heightened confidentiality and compliance needs — see `05-financial-automation-segments.md` for those specific, evidenced client profiles.

Unlike Human Center's Shrinking AI service, **no weighted ICP scoring model exists yet** for Automation Center's audience — this is a hypothesis-stage target definition pending real client interviews.

## Decisions / Rules
- Do not import Shrinking AI's A1-A4 scoring model uncritically; Automation Center's buyer is not necessarily the same "AI-frustrated consultant" persona and may be a larger, more process-mature firm.
- Do not price or position Automation Center as an enterprise/PE-fund-facing AI vendor generally; see `05-financial-automation-segments.md` for the specific rule governing investment-fund-adjacent clients.
