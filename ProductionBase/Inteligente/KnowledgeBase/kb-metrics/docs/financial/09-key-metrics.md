# Key Metrics

## Purpose
Define the metrics Inteligente should track once it begins operating.

## Scope
- In scope: candidate early-stage metrics, plus concrete near-term proof-asset targets named in research.
- Out of scope: actual measured values (none exist yet — pre-pilot).

## Current State
No metrics are instrumented yet. Two tiers of metrics apply:

**Near-term proof-asset targets (from research, concrete and named)** — the research explicitly identifies a lack of proof assets as an operational gap. Named targets to close it:
- **10+ completed paid sessions** (minimum sample before claiming a track record).
- **3 case write-ups** (documented client outcomes, suitable for the website/proof section).
- **A consent evidence pack** (signed client permissions to reference/quote their engagement — required before any case write-up can be published, and a prerequisite for the "demonstrated not claimed" positioning in `../../../kb-unfair-advantage/docs/strategy/10-unfair-advantage.md`).

**Candidate ongoing operating metrics for Shrinking AI (hypothesis, not yet instrumented)**:
- Free-empathy-call-to-paid-session conversion rate.
- Paid-session-to-retainer/implementation-support conversion rate.
- Average paid-session fee (pending pricing decision, see `../../../kb-revenue-streams/docs/model/07-revenue-streams.md`).
- Monthly recurring retainer revenue (for clients who take the optional follow-on).
- Time-to-delivery per session (target: within the 2-hour session-flow window).
- Consultant/freelancer (A4) referral rate, once the referral-partner hypothesis in `../../../kb-key-partners/docs/partnerships/06-key-partners.md` is tested.

**Candidate metrics for Automation Center (hypothesis, not yet instrumented)**, derived from the discovery-and-ROI methodology:
- Discovery-interview (or chatbot) completion rate and voluntary-abandonment rate.
- Distribution of qualification outcomes: Stop / Measure / Prototype / Pilot.
- Evidence-grade distribution (A-E) of intake data, as a proxy for prospect data readiness.
- Measurement-sprint-to-pilot conversion rate.
- Pilot-to-signed-contract conversion rate.
- Realized-vs-projected ROI variance, measured from matched pre/post pilot evidence (see `../../../kb-solution/docs/strategy/03-automation-center-solution.md`).
- Human-escalation rate and resolution time (legal/compliance/advice/incident triggers).

## Decisions / Rules
- Treat the three proof-asset targets as prerequisites for any public-facing credibility claim; do not publish case content without corresponding consent-pack evidence.
- Ongoing operating metrics remain a candidate list pending the first pilot, for both Shrinking AI and Automation Center.
- Automation Center's realized-vs-projected ROI variance must be computed from matched pre/post evidence, never from interview-stage estimates alone.
