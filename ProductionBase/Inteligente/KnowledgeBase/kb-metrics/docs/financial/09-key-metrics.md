# Key Metrics

## Purpose
Define candidate early-stage metrics, proof-asset targets, and pilot outcomes for Inteligente's current operations.

## Scope
- In scope: candidate early-stage metrics, concrete near-term proof-asset targets named in research, recorded evidence from completed Shrinking AI sessions, and measurement criteria for the Automation Center pilot.
- Out of scope: instrumented longitudinal time series or claimed internal Consultancy Automation or external Financial Automation pilot results (neither pilot has a recorded result).

## Current State
No operating metrics are instrumented as a series yet. Two initial Shrinking AI sessions are recorded. Client A's separate paid Expanding Human consultancy is now classified and reported at €50/60 minutes (n=1); it is not counted toward Shrinking AI's 10-session target or its €50/60-minute average (n=2). The external partner-led Financial Automation Discovery Pilot was formally initiated in Sept 2026 but is now deferred, not cancelled, until internal Consultancy Automation is validated; it still has no recorded first session or result and remains subject to the existing safety/data-flow gates. Two tiers of metrics apply:

**Founder-reported outputs and follow-up (2 client accounts; business impact remains unmeasured)**:
- **Client A, self-publishing writer**: pre-print/pre-publication materials were produced and sent; the client later confirmed using them for printing and publishing the book. Whether they caused publication or changed its quality, timing, or business outcome is unknown. The client then engaged in a separate Expanding Human consultancy (€50, 60 minutes) about AI use in the business beyond editing and formatting, receiving a business model canvas plus a "marketing scope of AI application." Consultant effort and any attributable outcome remain unknown. The client is considering Automation Center services; no order or delivery was reported.
- **Client B, self-employed tour guide**: a business-model canvas and business plan for website construction were delivered; the client has started building the website and expressed interest in ordering website-administration automation. No order or delivery was reported. Website completion, leads, bookings, and revenue are unknown.
- The writer's roughly month-long AI experimentation was client effort before consulting, not consultant delivery time. Consultant preparation/live/follow-up hours and elapsed delivery time remain unknown for the initial sessions.

**Near-term proof-asset targets (from research, concrete and named)** — the research explicitly identifies a lack of proof assets as an operational gap. Named targets to close it:
- **10+ completed paid sessions** — **2 counted of 10** from the initial Shrinking AI sessions (self-published writer, AI for e-book formatting; self-employed tour guide, AI for content creation; both €50/60-min). Client A's separate Expanding Human consultancy is excluded from this Shrinking AI target.
- **3 case write-ups** (documented client outcomes, suitable for the website/proof section) — not yet started; requires consent from the 2 completed clients first.
- **A consent evidence pack** (signed client permissions to reference/quote their engagement — required before any case write-up can be published, and a prerequisite for the "demonstrated not claimed" positioning in `../../../kb-unfair-advantage/docs/strategy/10-unfair-advantage.md`) — not yet collected for the 2 initial Shrinking AI sessions.
**Expanding Human observation (one reported paid session; not a stable metric)**:
- One separate €50/60-minute paid session is reported for Client A, with a business model canvas and "marketing scope of AI application" delivered.
- This n=1 observation is not counted toward Shrinking AI's 2-of-10 target or its €50 average-session fee, and does not establish a standard Expanding Human rate or repeatable method.
- Preparation/live/follow-up effort, client outcome, and repeat demand are not recorded.

**Candidate ongoing operating metrics for Shrinking AI (hypothesis, not yet instrumented)**:
- Free-empathy-call-to-paid-session conversion rate.
- Paid-session-to-retainer/implementation-support conversion rate.
- Average paid-session fee: **€50** (n=2, Sept 2026); too small a sample to be a stable metric yet, see `../../../kb-revenue-streams/docs/model/07-revenue-streams.md`.
- Monthly recurring retainer revenue (for clients who take the optional follow-on).
- Time-to-delivery per session (target: within the 2-hour session-flow window).
- Consultant/freelancer (A4) referral rate, once the referral-partner hypothesis in `../../../kb-key-partners/docs/partnerships/06-key-partners.md` is tested.

**Candidate metrics for Automation Center (hypothesis, not yet instrumented)**, derived from the discovery-and-ROI methodology:
- Need-based referral/qualification/order/outcome counts in both directions (Human Center → Automation Center and Automation Center → Shrinking AI/Expanding Human), reported separately by direction. The routing is a strategy, not demonstrated conversion; two Human-Center-originating Automation Center interest signals remain unqualified and no reverse-direction conversion is recorded.
- For planned internal Consultancy Automation, define workflow-specific, comparable pre/post efficiency and outcome measures only after the internal workflow and baseline are selected. No internal workflow, hosting, baseline, or outcome data is recorded yet; internal validation must precede any external Financial Automation pilot.
- Discovery-interview (or chatbot) completion rate and voluntary-abandonment rate.
- Pilot-method validation, including required-question coverage, evidence-grade distribution, whether follow-up prompts resolved uncertainty, and founder correction/rejection rate for the draft plan and ROI range.
- Commercial-pilot outcome: documented buying decision or qualified paid-pilot offer; actual payment is tracked separately.
- Distribution of qualification outcomes: Stop / Measure / Prototype / Pilot.
- Evidence-grade distribution (A-E) of intake data, as a proxy for prospect data readiness.
- Measurement-sprint-to-pilot conversion rate.
- Pilot-to-signed-contract conversion rate.
- Realized-vs-projected ROI variance, measured from matched pre/post pilot evidence (see `../../../kb-solution/docs/strategy/03-automation-center-solution.md`).
- Human-escalation rate and resolution time (legal/compliance/advice/incident triggers).

## Decisions / Rules
- Treat the three proof-asset targets as prerequisites for any public-facing credibility claim; do not publish case content without corresponding consent-pack evidence.
- Treat ongoing operating metrics as candidates until definitions and instrumentation are established; the Shrinking AI €50 average is based on only two sessions and is not a trend. Consultancy Automation has not been run; the external Financial Automation partner pilot is deferred and remains blocked by its safety/data-flow gates.
- Do not record the two founder-reported Human-Center-originated Automation Center expressions of interest as orders, revenue, or validated conversion; no order or delivery was reported. Need-based routing in either direction remains a strategy until measured outcomes exist.
- Do not report pilot success until the interview/ROI workflow is validated and a buying decision or qualified paid-pilot offer is documented; payment remains a separate milestone.
- Automation Center's realized-vs-projected ROI variance must be computed from matched pre/post evidence, never from interview-stage estimates alone.
