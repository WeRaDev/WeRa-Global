## 3. Market structure (Lisbon → Sintra corridor)

### 3.1 Demand shape

- Lisbon OTA “things to do” first pages behave as **day-trip markets**, with **Sintra combinations** occupying top organic slots (public market notes, Oct 2026).
- Budget **group** Sintra combos often list **from ~€23–€35** “from” prices — frequently **excluding** full palace interiors or bundling lightly (ticket reality check: Pena park/palace adult tickets commonly ~€12–€20 class on Parques de Sintra channels).
- **Private** products cluster higher: roughly **€65–€150+/person** on aggregator comparison pages (Nov 2026 samples), tickets often extra unless “all-inclusive” packaging.
- Group-size private full-days sometimes priced **per vehicle** (e.g. ~€285–€600+ for 1–4 pax class examples) — different economic logic than InCity’s **per-adult** resume bands.

### 3.2 Price ladder (directional, public listings)

| Tier | Format | Indicative public “from” | What guests buy |
|------|--------|---------------------------|-----------------|
| Budget OTA group | Shared bus/minivan day trip | ~€24–€50 | Coverage of stops; high review volume; lower customization |
| Mid small-group | 8–10 pax caps | ~€60–€110 | Slightly better pace; still OTA-led |
| Private mid | Private car/van, tickets often extra | ~€70–€130/pp | Flexibility; hotel pickup; guide quality |
| Private “tickets-in” | Pena (and sometimes more) included | ~€88–€154/pp samples | Lower planning friction; stronger conversion |
| Premium private | Awards/lux packaging, planning calls, all-in | often €150+ / group day fees | Care narrative, photo help, multi-day upsell |

**InCity internal bands (A1–A3 ~€80–€130 adult; express/palace combos higher)** sit in **private mid**, competitive with Picta / Host Experiences / I Go Portugal-class listings — **if** discoverability and bookability match. Without OTA rank and instant book, those prices are invisible to most shoppers.

### 3.3 Portugal context (macro, not Sintra-only)

Public industry digests (2025–2026) describe Portugal tourism as high-volume and fragmented among many registered operators; heritage/cultural day products remain a major Lisbon-area draw. Use macro only as backdrop: **InCity competes in a crowded Sintra day-trip shelf**, not in multi-day national circuit brands.

### 4.2 Indirect / shelf-shaping competitors

- **High-volume GYG/Viator group Sintra combos** — set guest price expectations and soak review mass.
- **Parques de Sintra timed tickets** — capacity and queue mechanics; operators who pre-book slots reduce guest anxiety.
- **Self-guided + train + tuk-tuk** — substitutes for couples optimizing cost; InCity must sell *why private car + guide* beats DIY.

## 6. Positioning map (where InCity can win)

```text
                    High customization
                           ^
                           |
     InCity*  -------------+-------------  Yes You Deserve / Picta premium
     (story + Goodfoot +   |              (process, awards, planning)
      solo face)           |
                           |
  Low price <--------------+--------------> High price / all-in
                           |
     OTA group buses       |   Ticket-included private fleets
     (€24–€50 shelf)       |   (SWINGO, Duca flagship SKUs)
                           v
                    Low customization
```

\*InCity’s natural cell: **high customization, mid private price, guide-as-brand**.  
It loses when guests never see the cell because discovery defaults to OTA group shelf.

**Claim hierarchy (recommended for site + ads + OTA titles):**

1. Private day, only your group  
2. Flexible route Lisbon **or** Sintra start  
3. Story-led local guide (languages explicit)  
4. Optional Goodfoot / food upgrades  
5. Theatrical character / convertible — **supporting color**, not the only headline

## 7. Marketing strategy implications (actionable)

### 7.1 Phase 1 (brochure site live — now)

| Priority | Action | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| P0 | Finish Home/Contact copy via Content Matrix; apply checklist; re-verify | Site is now a ranking/trust asset; weak copy wastes HC-023 |
| P0 | Google Business Profile: ops city Lisbon/Sintra/Cascais, photos, WhatsApp, website link | Fixes discovery leak; reviews migrate off FB |
| P0 | One primary CTA path (WhatsApp + form) with response SLA | Solo ops live/die on speed-to-lead |
| P1 | Three public packages only (e.g. Classic full-day, Short, Sintra-start half-day) + custom | Catalog is too wide for conversion |
| P1 | “From €X / adult” on each package | Matches every serious private peer |
| P1 | Testimonials page: pull multilingual FB/OTA quotes with consent | Trust transfer |
| P2 | GYG/Viator product audit: live SKUs, photos, title = private day-trip not only costume | Capture OTA intent |
| P2 | EN primary; FR/ES/PT landing blocks | Audience mix from prior research |
| Later | Booking engine (ADR 0003) only after lead volume justifies commission math | Avoid premature stack cost |

### 7.2 Message tests (for ads/site heroes)

- A: “Private Sintra & coast day — your pace, your stops.”  
- B: “Lisbon or Sintra start. One guide. No strangers on the tour.”  
- C: “Story-led private days — optional park-care Goodfoot time.”  

Do **not** lead with AI YouTube or costume-only until conversion baseline exists.

### 7.3 Economics reminder (for founder conversations)

- OTA commission often **~20–30%** of OTA gross.  
- Engine SaaS often **tens of €/mo + %** class.  
- InCity’s value of **direct WhatsApp/site leads** is high; brochure phase is correctly sequenced.  
- Inteligente should not promise OTA rank or ROI; can help package, copy, admin process, and later stack choice.
