# InCity / Invisible City Tours — Business profile and audience

**Status:** sanitized research synthesis for Inteligente internal use  
**Client codename:** InCity  
**Operating brand:** Invisible City Tours  
**Derived from:** `Customers/Vasco Da Lama/` Facebook deep-study and desk-research notes  
**Date packaged:** 2026-10-05

## Business identity
- Solo-operator private tour business led by guide **Vasco** (public brand face for Invisible City Tours).
- Facebook positioning historically emphasized "historical tours with an historical character" (EN/FR), categorized as a historical tour agency at a budget tier.
- Reviewed/delivered product is primarily **private customizable day trips** in the Lisbon–Cascais–Sintra–Cabo da Roca corridor, often with private transport (convertible noted in reviews), storytelling, and itinerary flexibility — not a mass walking-tour product.
- Administrative location signal: Cebolais de Cima (Castelo Branco district) while operational theater is the Lisbon corridor — local-search/Maps confusion risk.
- At research time: no owned website and no public structured booking system; contact via phone and personal email (see internal dossier for values).
- Related client creative framing: DEEPSINTRA YouTube / Invisible City Lab notes describe experimental AI-generated historical reconstruction video and regenerative tourism messaging. Treat as adjacent brand experiment, not the validated core offer.

## Audience and demand signals
### Facebook follower demographics (page insights; imperfect proxy)
- Age skew: ~64% aged 45+ (largest band 45–54).
- Country mix: Portugal largest, then France, USA, Italy, Belgium, Spain.
- City mix skewed to Castelo Branco over Lisbon — follower base likely inflated by operator regional network vs pure tourist demand.
- Engagement weak relative to review strength: stagnant followers (~850), low reactions/comments/shares, views partly from non-followers via shared links.

### Paying-client profile inferred from reviews
- Couples and small friend/family groups more than backpackers or coach groups.
- Strong French-speaking and Spanish/Latin American segments plus EN-speaking US/UK guests.
- Multilingual guiding repeatedly cited (reports of ~5 languages).
- Praise clusters: personalization/flexibility; off-path stops; guide personality; value for money; comfort touches and local food tips.
- Trust asset: ~95 Facebook reviews at 100% recommend (as of source research). Near-zero negative review pattern reported.

## Strategic interpretation
1. Real product-market fit sits in **personalized private heritage day trips**, not costume-only positioning.
2. Facebook followers are a weak demand proxy; reviews are the stronger commercial asset.
3. Digital funnel is underbuilt vs competitors (no transparent public booking site/OTA stack at research time).
4. Earlier AI YouTube push underperformed vs expected engagement — aligns with Inteligente Shrinking AI session redirect toward website/booking path.
