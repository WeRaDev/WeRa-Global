1. Project objective

For InCity: grow online presence and receive more tour bookings through a clear website and connected channels, with one place to manage the calendar and avoid double bookings.

For Inteligente: deliver InCity as the first pilot of a repeatable “website + booking operations” service for tourism businesses (starting in Sintra), under a partnership model that keeps setup affordable.

Design reference: structure inspired by established Sintra operators such as Mr. Local (clear packages, guide-centred story, transparent “from” pricing, strong book CTA)—filled with InCity / Invisible City Tours content and Vasco’s real private day-trip offer.

2. Launch scope (what is included)

Included at launch

  • Odoo-branded public website (EN first; PT/FR/ES can be phased if agreed)
  • Site information architecture inspired by Mr. Local: Home, Tours, About/Guide, Testimonials, Contact
  • 3–5 simplified public tour packages (not the full internal catalogue on day one)
  • Embedded booking experience powered by a professional tour booking engine (default: Bókun or equivalent)
  • Instant online book & pay on the direct website channel
  • Central booking calendar in the engine (single inventory truth)
  • Viator connection with shared availability to reduce double-booking risk
  • WhatsApp / phone path retained for custom private requests
  • Basic operator runbook (close days, offline booking entry, daily checks)
  • Basic website copywriting for launch pages (package blurbs, about, key CTAs)—not unlimited brand campaigns
  • Website hosted on Inteligente-operated infrastructure (Frank self-hosted Odoo)
  • Hosting and care under the monthly partnership subscription (see Pricing)

Explicitly not promised at launch

  • A custom “Bókun replacement”, AI tourism agent, or MCP-based multi-platform automation
  • Nextcloud as the live booking system of record (optional later research only)
  • Full multi-OTA day-one set (GetYourGuide, Booking.com Experiences, etc.)—can be phased after Viator is stable
  • Publishing the entire historical tour SKU list
  • Guaranteed booking volume or ROI
Important: OTA marketplace commissions (typically a percentage of each OTA booking) are charged by those platforms to the operator as usual. They are not removed by this partnership. The monthly fee is designed to cover the booking-engine platform subscription (Bókun-class), not OTA commissions.

3. Booking journey

A. Direct (website)

Guest opens InCity site → chooses package & date → pays online → instant confirmation → seat blocked in central calendar → operator notified.

B. Viator

Guest books on Viator → booking flows into the same engine calendar → availability updates → operator fulfils as usual (per Viator rules).

C. Instagram / YouTube

Social posts and channel links point to the website checkout (or a specific tour page)—not a separate calendar.

D. Offline / WhatsApp

Custom or phone deals are entered into the central calendar so channels stay consistent.

Payment on direct channel: instant online checkout. Payment and cancellation on OTAs: according to each platform’s rules.

Setup (one-time)
€500
Website build/customisation, basic launch copywriting, and initial engine & Viator configuration. Reference value of a full bespoke project is ~€5,000; pilot rate is 10% as partnership contribution. Domain registration is paid separately by InCity.
Monthly subscription
€50 / mo
Frank website hosting, maintenance/care, and Bókun-class engine subscription fee (~€50 band) covered under this pilot subscription.

What €50 / month includes

  • Website hosting on Frank and routine maintenance/care within agreed limits
  • Booking-engine platform subscription (Bókun or equivalent) for this pilot—priced to cover that engine fee
  • Reasonable configuration support for the launch stack

What €50 / month does not include

  • OTA commissions (e.g. Viator percentage on OTA sales)—paid via those platforms as standard
  • Payment-processor fees on transactions (Stripe/PayPal/etc.), unless explicitly agreed otherwise
  • Major redesigns, new custom features, extra languages beyond launch agreement, or additional OTA onboarding beyond Viator without a change order
  • Paid ads, professional photography shoots, or legal/tax advice
Cost path: the €50 monthly fee is set to cover the Bókun-class engine subscription for this pilot, plus Frank hosting/care. OTA commissions remain outside the fee. If/when Inteligente later operates its own booking engine, provider infrastructure cost may fall—InCity’s published pilot price does not automatically change unless both parties agree in writing.

8. Referral programme

If InCity introduces other tour operators who become active paying subscribers of the same Inteligente tourism website+booking service:

  • Reward: InCity’s own €50 monthly subscription becomes free (service credit only) once 5 referred customers are simultaneously active and paying.
  • Credit applies only to InCity’s subscription—not a cash payout in this v1 rule.
  • A referred customer counts when their subscription is active and not in trial/default.
  • If active referred subscribers fall below 5, the free credit pauses until the threshold is met again (unless a later written rule says otherwise).

Example: 5 active referred subscribers → InCity pays €0/mo subscription credit; OTA commissions and other exclusions still apply as above.

9. Future development (not launch scope)

Inteligente may later invest in a proprietary booking layer, assistant/agent integrations, or deeper automation for tourism operators. Those initiatives:

  • Are not acceptance criteria for InCity launch
  • Do not guarantee removal of third-party tools on a fixed date
  • Remain Inteligente product decisions, with migration only by mutual agreement if it affects InCity’s live service

1. Objetivo do projeto

Para a InCity: reforçar a presença online e receber mais reservas de tours com um website claro e canais ligados, com um sítio central para gerir o calendário e evitar reservas em duplicado.

Para a Inteligente: entregar a InCity como primeiro piloto de um serviço repetível de “website + operações de reserva” para negócios de turismo (começando em Sintra), num modelo de parceria com setup acessível.

Referência de design: estrutura inspirada em operadores estabelecidos em Sintra (ex.: Mr. Local)—pacotes claros, história centrada no guia, preços “a partir de”, CTA de reserva—com conteúdo próprio da Invisible City Tours.

2. Âmbito de lançamento (o que está incluído)

Incluído no lançamento

  • Website público em Odoo (EN primeiro; PT/FR/ES podem ser faseados se acordado)
  • Arquitetura de páginas inspirada no Mr. Local: Início, Tours, Sobre/Guia, Testemunhos, Contacto

  • Experiência de reserva incorporada com motor profissional (predefinição: Bókun ou equivalente)

  • Calendário central no motor (única fonte de verdade do inventário)
  • Ligação à Viator com disponibilidade partilhada para reduzir double-booking
  • WhatsApp / telefone mantidos para pedidos privados personalizados
  • Guia operativo básico para o guia

Explicitamente não prometido no lançamento

  • Nextcloud como sistema oficial de reservas no lançamento
  • Todas as OTAs no dia um (GetYourGuide, Booking.com Experiences, etc.)

Importante: as comissões das OTAs (percentagem sobre cada reserva na plataforma) continuam a ser cobradas ao operador como é habitual. A mensalidade cobre a subscrição do motor de reservas (tipo Bókun), não as comissões das OTAs.

7. Termos da parceria (resumo)

  • IP do produto reutilizável / motor: propriedade da Inteligente.
  • Marca e conteúdo: propriedade da InCity (incluindo media fornecida pela InCity).
  • Domínio: a InCity paga o registo/DNS e é titular do domínio. A Inteligente pode ajudar a apontar o DNS para o site em Frank.

  • Textos de lançamento: o setup inclui copywriting básico; projetos editoriais maiores ficam fora de âmbito salvo acordo.

  • Contas: preferência por logins da InCity; Inteligente com acesso mínimo necessário.
  • Cancelamento: aviso escrito sugerido de 30 dias para a mensalidade; setup não reembolsável após início dos trabalhos, salvo acordo.

8. Programa de referência

Quando a InCity trouxer outros operadores de tours que sejam subscritores ativos e a pagar o mesmo serviço Inteligente:

  • Com 5 referidos ativos em simultâneo, a mensalidade de €50 da InCity fica grátis (apenas crédito de serviço).
  • Não é pagamento em dinheiro nesta regra v1.
  • Se o número de referidos ativos descer abaixo de 5, o crédito pausa até voltar ao limiar.
