1. Project objective

For InCity: put a professional website online so tourists who find you on social media can learn about the tours and contact the guide. The guide continues to schedule tours manually (phone, WhatsApp, email, or other personal channels).

For Inteligente: deliver a focused brochure + lead-capture site hosted on Frank, as the agreed first deliverable after client conversation (2026-11-09).

Scope change vs earlier draft: this v2 does not include online booking, payments, or connections to Viator / Tripadvisor / Booking / GetYourGuide. Those remain optional later projects.

2. What is included / excluded

Included

  • Public website hosted on Frank and available on the internet
  • Pages inspired by clear tour-operator structure (e.g. Home, Tours, About/Guide, Contact)—filled with InCity content
  • Presentation of selected tours (informative: description, duration, indicative info—not a live booking cart)
  • Lead capture / communication: contact paths so visitors can reach the guide (e.g. form and/or WhatsApp / phone / email CTAs)
  • Social lead routing: website ready so YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram can link/redirect people to the site (profile/bio/link-in-bio, video descriptions, posts)
  • Basic launch copywriting from your facts and photos
  • Guidance checklist for placing links on YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram
  • Free hosting for 3 months from go-live (see Pricing)

Excluded (not part of this €300 project)

  • Online booking engine or calendar with automatic confirmation
  • Payment channels (Stripe, PayPal, card checkout, etc.)
  • Integration with tourism platforms (Viator, Tripadvisor Experiences, Booking.com, GetYourGuide, etc.)
  • Automatic tour scheduling or multi-channel inventory sync
  • Monthly subscription for this phase (none)
  • Paid ads management, professional photo shoots, or ongoing social media content production

3. How visitors reach you

YouTube

Channel / video descriptions and about links point to the website (and ideally a contact or tours page).

Facebook

Page button, about link, and posts send interested people to the website.

Instagram

Link in bio / stories stickers send followers to the website.

On the website

Visitor reads about you and the tours, then contacts the guide. You schedule the tour manually.

“Integration” here means clear, working redirects and CTAs—not API automation of those social platforms.

7. Partnership terms (summary)

  • Site templates / reusable methods: Inteligente may reuse general patterns for other clients.
  • Your brand, photos, and tour texts: remain InCity’s.
  • Domain: if you pay for it, you own it.

1. Objetivo do projeto

Para a InCity: ter um website profissional online para quem o encontra nas redes sociais conhecer os tours e contactar o guia. O guia continua a marcar os tours manualmente.

Para a Inteligente: entregar um site de apresentação + captura de contactos alojado em Frank, conforme conversa com o cliente (2026-11-09).

Alteração de âmbito: esta v2 não inclui reservas online, pagamentos nem ligações a Viator / Tripadvisor / Booking / GetYourGuide.

2. O que está incluído / excluído

Incluído

  • Website público em Frank, acessível na internet
  • Páginas de apresentação (Início, Tours, Sobre, Contacto—nomes podem variar)
  • Informação sobre tours selecionados (sem carrinho de reservas)
  • Comunicação / leads: formulário e/ou WhatsApp / telefone / email
  • Redes sociais: site preparado para YouTube, Facebook e Instagram enviarem visitantes ao website
  • Copywriting básico de lançamento

Excluído deste projeto de €300

  • Motor de reservas online ou confirmação automática
  • Canais de pagamento
  • Integração com plataformas de turismo (Viator, etc.)
  • Agendamento automático multi-canal
  • Subscrição mensal nesta fase
  • Gestão de anúncios ou produção contínua de conteúdo social

3. Como os visitantes chegam

YouTube, Facebook e Instagram ligam para o website. No site, o visitante lê e contacta o guia. O guia marca o tour manualmente. “Integração” = ligações e CTAs claros, não APIs das redes sociais.
